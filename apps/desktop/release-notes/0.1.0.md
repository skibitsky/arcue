The first release of the Arcue desktop app, for macOS, Windows and Linux.

Arcue is where you and your agent review 3D models and animations side by side. Comment right on the model at any frame, and your agent gets the exact vertices, bones, frame, camera and a screenshot. It opens FBX and Blender files.

- Pin or paint comments on the model, on one frame, a range or the whole model. They stay in place when you re-export.
- FBX and `.blend` files, with animation files, props on bones, LODs, collision and sockets in one view.
- Live reload on every export, with reviews kept in `.arcue/` in your repository.
- The `arcue` command: `arcue open` shows a model at a clip and frame, `arcue inspect --json` reads a file, and `arcue serve` runs Arcue in a browser.
